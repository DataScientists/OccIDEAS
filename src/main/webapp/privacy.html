Privacy notice

OccIDEAS workplace exposure questionnaire • Last updated 25 September 2026

Who we are

This questionnaire is run by OccIDEAS Pty Ltd (ABN 77 645 436 915). You can contact us about privacy at privacy@occideas.org. For general enquiries, contact info@occideas.org.

What this questionnaire does

It asks about the work you do and uses expert assessment rules to estimate your exposure to workplace hazards. It is an estimate of your exposure at work. It does not assess your health or diagnose any condition.

What we collect

  • Your job title and a description of your usual tasks
  • Your answers to the questionnaire (tasks, materials, working conditions and protective measures)
  • Your estimated exposure results
  • A participant ID that we create for you
  • Your employer's code, if you enter one

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, address or date of birth, and you don't need to log in. Please don't include names or other personal details in your answers.

Why we collect it

To choose the right questions for your job, estimate your exposure, and give you your report. If you entered an employer code, your information is also used for OccIDEAS's report to your employer (see below).

Your answers may be health information

Information about exposure to workplace hazards may count as health information under the Privacy Act 1988. We handle it with the extra care that requires, and we only collect it with your consent. By completing the questionnaire, you consent to us collecting and using your information as described in this notice.

Who we share it with

  • Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS). Your job title and task description are sent to the ABS occupation coding service to match your occupation. Nothing else is sent to the ABS.
  • Your employer, but only if you entered their code and ticked the consent box. OccIDEAS prepares a report for your employer, as arranged with them before the code was issued. Your employer may see your answers and will be told if your estimated exposure to a hazard is above the exposure limit, so they can take steps to reduce the risk. Your employer is not told which results are yours.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS), which hosts the servers where your information is stored.

We don't sell your information or use it for marketing.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored on Amazon Web Services servers in Australia.

How we protect it

Your information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and access is limited to authorised OccIDEAS staff.

How long we keep it

  • If you entered an employer code: we keep your information for the period agreed with your employer for that study. To find out the period for your study, ask your employer or contact us.
  • If you took part without a code: we keep your information for 7 years, then delete it.

Seeing or deleting your information

Because we don't know who you are, we can only find your answers using your participant ID. Your participant ID is shown on your report. To ask to see, correct or delete your information, contact us at privacy@occideas.org with your participant ID.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, please contact us first at privacy@occideas.org. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

This questionnaire gives an estimate of your exposure at work. It is not a health assessment or medical diagnosis.
