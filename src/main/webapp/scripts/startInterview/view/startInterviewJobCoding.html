Could your work be affecting your health?

Many jobs involve exposure to things like dusts, fumes, chemicals, noise or radiation. Health and safety professionals know these hazards well, but it takes time for research to become common knowledge. Asbestos, for example, was linked to lung disease in the 1920s, but wasn't banned in Australia until 2003. That's why many people aren't aware of the risks in their own work.

This short questionnaire asks about the work you do and checks it against known workplace health hazards. At the end you'll get a personal summary of any exposures that may apply to you, and what they mean.
