Workplace Exposure Questionnaire

Estimates your occupational exposure to known workplace health hazards

Many jobs involve exposure to things like dusts, fumes, chemicals, noise or radiation. Health and safety professionals know these hazards well, but it takes time for research to become common knowledge, so many people aren't aware of the health hazards in their own work. Asbestos, for example, was linked to lung disease in the 1920s, but wasn't banned in Australia until 2003.

This short questionnaire asks about the work you do and checks it against known workplace health hazards. At the end you'll get a personal summary of your estimated exposures, and what they mean.

  • Takes about 5–15 minutes, depending on the kinds of tasks you do
  • No login or contact details needed
  • An estimate of your exposure at work, not a health assessment or diagnosis
