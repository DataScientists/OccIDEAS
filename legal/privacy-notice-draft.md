## Privacy notice

**Who we are.** This questionnaire is run by OccIDEAS Pty Ltd (ABN 77 645 436 915). You can contact us
about privacy at privacy@occideas.org. For general enquiries, contact info@occideas.org.

**What this questionnaire does.** It asks about the work you do and uses expert assessment rules to
estimate your exposure to workplace hazards. It does not assess your health or diagnose any condition.

**What we collect.**
- Your job title and a description of your usual tasks
- Your answers to the questionnaire (tasks, materials, working conditions and protective measures)
- Your estimated exposure results
- A participant ID that we create for you
- If you enter one, your employer's code

We do **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number, address or date of birth, and you don't
need to log in. Please don't include names or other personal details in your answers.

**Why we collect it.** To choose the right questions for your job, estimate your exposure, and give you
your report. If you entered an employer code, it is also used for OccIDEAS's report to your employer
(see below).

**Your answers may be health information.** Information about exposure to workplace hazards may count
as health information under the *Privacy Act 1988*. We handle it with the extra care that requires, and
we only collect it with your consent. By completing the questionnaire, you consent to us collecting and
using it as described in this notice.

*Note to advisor: is completing the questionnaire enough consent to collect sensitive information, or
should there be a tick box before the interview starts for everyone, not only employer-code participants?*

**Who we share it with.**
- **Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS).** Your job title and task description are sent to the ABS
  occupation coding service to match your occupation. Nothing else is sent to the ABS.
- **Your employer, only if you entered their code and ticked the consent box.** OccIDEAS prepares a
  report for your employer, as arranged with them before the code was issued. Your employer may see your
  answers and will be told if your estimated exposure to a hazard is above the exposure limit, so they
  can take steps to reduce the risk. Your employer is not told which results are yours.
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS),** which hosts the servers where your information is stored.

We don't sell your information or use it for marketing.

**Where it is stored.** Your information is stored on Amazon Web Services servers in Australia.

*Note to advisor: if any data is stored or processed outside Australia (including by the hosting
provider or the ABS coding service's infrastructure), APP 8 disclosure may be needed. We need to confirm
these locations.*

**How we protect it.** Your information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and access is limited
to authorised OccIDEAS staff.

**How long we keep it.**
- **If you entered an employer code:** we keep your information for the period agreed with your employer
  for that study. To find out the period for your study, ask your employer or contact us.
- **If you took part without a code:** we keep your information for 7 years, then delete it.

**Seeing or deleting your information.** Because we don't know who you are, we can only find your
answers using your participant ID. Your participant ID is shown on your report. To ask to see, correct or delete your information, contact
privacy@occideas.org with your participant ID.

*Note to advisor: the report (and its PDF) now shows "Your participant ID: … Keep this if you want to
contact us about your data." Is that enough for participants to use their access and deletion rights?*

**Complaints.** If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, contact us first at
privacy@occideas.org. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office
of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at [www.oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).
